# Scotland commercial expansion intent map

Decision recorded: 1 August 2026

## Pricing decision

The existing £299 package combines an AI visibility check with technical SEO, structured data, entity, llms, and recommendation work. It will be named consistently as the standard AI Visibility and AEO Audit rather than being silently repriced.

The introductory £99 AI Visibility Audit is a separate, narrower customer-acquisition offer. It includes representative AI and search observations, a technical and entity-level website review, a small competitor visibility sample, prioritised findings, and a concise written action plan. Implementation and ongoing management are excluded. The offer is introductory and subject to limited availability, with no invented closing date or outcome guarantee.

## Cannibalisation controls

AI Visibility pages own measurement, observation, citations, recommendations, and entity representation. Answer Engine Optimisation pages own implementation, extraction, answer formatting, schema, and canonical search architecture. Automation owns repeated workflows. Deterministic assistants own controlled answers from approved knowledge. Internal systems own operational interfaces and connected state. Bespoke software owns wider custom application delivery for Scottish SMEs.
