# Scotland search expansion route decision

Decision recorded: 1 August 2026

## Evidence boundary

This decision combines repository evidence with a public search-result review completed on 1 August 2026. Search-result competition is not keyword volume. No authorised keyword-volume or cost-per-click source was available, so neither was measured.

## Supporting editorial decisions

Build the four requested Insights because they address educational and sector-specific questions rather than provider-selection intent. Do not create a second McCaig's Tower case study. The existing case study already documents why the structured visitor system exists, its architecture, its evidence limits, and the lessons that can be generalised.

## Duplicate-intent controls

Each commercial route has one primary intent. Provider-selection pages focus on choosing an implementation partner. Explanatory pages define the discipline and implementation model. AI visibility pages focus on observation, source accuracy, and monitoring. AI systems pages focus on maintained operational applications. Automation pages focus on repeatable workflows and handoffs. Regional pages use regional operating conditions and evidence, not substituted place names.
