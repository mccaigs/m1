Getting ready for AI search starts with a simple question: can a person or machine understand your business quickly from the public website?

If the answer is no, adding more technology will not fix the problem. The website needs clearer source material first.

## Start with the services

Many websites describe services in broad language. That can be fine for brand tone, but it is weak for answer engines. AI systems need plain signals:

- What does the business do?
- Who is it for?
- Where does it operate?
- What problems does it solve?
- What makes it credible?
- What should a customer do next?

Each important service should have enough detail to stand alone.

## Improve structure before volume

Publishing more pages is not automatically useful. A small number of clear, accurate pages usually beats a large set of thin, overlapping pages.

Useful structure includes headings that match real questions, internal links between related topics, concise summaries, FAQs, and contact routes. The site should help a visitor move from a broad problem to a specific next step.

## Add machine-readable signals

Structured data helps search and AI systems interpret the page. It should be used carefully. Organisation, service, breadcrumb, article, and FAQ schema can all be useful when they reflect the visible page content.

An llms.txt file can also provide a concise AI-readable guide to the business, services, key pages, and preferred description. It should support the website, not contradict it.

## Check crawlability

AI readiness still depends on technical basics. Important pages should be indexable, present in the sitemap, and not blocked by robots rules. The site should be fast, responsive, and accessible.

If the pages are difficult to crawl or understand, AI visibility work has a weak foundation.

## Test carefully

Prompt testing can show how AI systems currently describe the business, but the results should be treated as evidence, not certainty. The useful question is not "did we get mentioned once?" It is "what source material is missing or unclear?"
