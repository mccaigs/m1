Answer Engine Optimisation is the work of making a business easier for AI-assisted search systems and answer engines to understand.

Traditional SEO helps people find your website. Answer Engine Optimisation helps AI systems understand what your business does, who it helps, where it operates, and why it may be a credible recommendation.

That distinction matters because modern search is no longer only a list of blue links. Customers may ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI, Perplexity, or another assistant for a shortlist, comparison, explanation, or recommendation. If your website is vague, thin, poorly structured, or difficult to crawl, the business becomes harder to understand.

## What AEO actually improves

AEO is not a trick and it is not a guaranteed route into an AI answer. It is a practical improvement programme for the source material AI systems may read, summarise, and compare.

Useful work can include:

- Clear service pages
- Accurate page titles and descriptions
- Structured data that reflects the real business
- Strong internal links between related services
- FAQs that answer real buyer questions
- Location and sector clarity
- Better crawlability
- llms.txt and llms-full.txt
- Consistent entity signals across the site

The aim is to reduce ambiguity.

## Why entity clarity matters

An answer engine needs to understand the entity behind the website. That means the business name, location, service areas, services, audience, proof points, and contact routes should be clear.

For a Scottish business, the difference between serving Edinburgh, Scotland, the UK, or a specific local market should not be left to guesswork. The website should state it plainly and connect that context across relevant pages.

## AEO still needs good SEO

Answer Engine Optimisation does not replace SEO. The fundamentals still matter: fast pages, useful content, indexable routes, readable headings, metadata, internal linking, and a sensible sitemap.

The difference is emphasis. AEO asks whether a machine can form an accurate summary of the business from the public website.

That is where [AI Visibility](/ai-visibility) becomes useful. AEO improves the source material. AI Visibility checks whether that source material is clear enough across prompts, entities, structured data, FAQs, and crawlable files.
